---

## Purpose
Turn one proposed AI capability into a falsifiable service experiment (history H01).

## Non-goals
No revenue promises, inferred customers, financial projections or automatic outreach.

## Inputs
One user problem, target audience, proposed deliverable, constraints and available evidence.

## Tools
Use existing project notes and a bounded experiment plan; contact people only within an authorized task.

## Procedure
1. Describe the problem and one concrete deliverable a recipient can inspect.
2. Record assumptions separately from observations and choose an observable success criterion.
3. Design the smallest manual trial that tests usefulness and willingness to pay.
4. Record actual responses and delivery effort; do not treat interest as a purchase.
5. Automate repeated steps only after demand and delivery quality have supporting evidence.

## Approval boundaries
A planning task does not authorize outreach, purchases, subscriptions or commitments.

## Validation
The experiment has explicit evidence for continue/change/stop and a demonstrable deliverable. Missing evidence remains unknown.

## Failure behavior
If the final artifact or response is unavailable, report planning only. A diagram or proposed funnel is not business success.

## Output
Problem, deliverable, assumptions, trial, acceptance evidence and next bounded step.
